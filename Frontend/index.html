ABOUT

The Classroom Scheduling System is designed to assist coordinators in managing classroom reservations for students and teachers in an organized and efficient manner. Its main purpose is to prevent conflicts, overlapping reservations, and confusion in room assignments by providing a centralized platform where all requests and schedules can be monitored and controlled properly.

Through this system, coordinators can check room availability, review and approve requests, and assign appropriate time slots based on both classroom availability and teachers’ schedules. It ensures that every class, meeting, or academic activity is given a proper room without affecting other reservations. By automating the scheduling process, the system reduces manual errors, avoids double-booking, and makes classroom management more efficient and reliable.

How It Works?

1. Coordinator logs into the system.

The coordinator accesses the system through the login page using their account credentials.

2. The coordinator dashboard is displayed.

After logging in, the system shows a dashboard where the coordinator can view schedules, requests, and room availability.

3. Students and teachers submit requests.

Students and teachers send classroom reservation requests, including their preferred time, room, and schedule.

4. The coordinator reviews requests.

The coordinator checks each request, considering room availability, teacher schedules, and existing bookings.

5. Requests are approved or adjusted.

The coordinator approves requests or makes necessary adjustments to avoid conflicts and ensure proper scheduling.

6. Approved schedules are recorded.

Once approved, reservations are added to the official classroom schedule stored in the system.

7. The updated schedule is visible.

Students and teachers can view the updated schedule to check room assignments and availability.

8. The system prevents conflicts.

Because all reservations are managed by the coordinator in one system, scheduling conflicts and double bookings are minimized.
